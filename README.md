# Paglu Game – Word Chain Challenge

## About the Game
Paglu Game is a fun and engaging single-player word challenge designed to test your vocabulary, speed, and quick thinking. In this game, you play against an AI opponent by forming words where each new word must begin with the last letter of the previous word.

What looks simple at first quickly becomes a real challenge as time runs out and the difficulty increases. Every move counts, and smart thinking is the key to winning.

With its unique riddle-style gameplay, Paglu Game offers an exciting mix of vocabulary building, strategy, and fast decision-making that keeps players engaged.
